The interface follows a Modern Minimalist + Humanitarian Healthcare design aesthetic with a clean, premium, and trustworthy appearance. The layout emphasizes generous whitespace, soft rounded corners, and subtle visual hierarchy to create an approachable and compassionate user experience. The overall style feels elegant, calm, and accessible rather than overly decorative.
